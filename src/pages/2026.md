# 2026 Winner: [Cursor Camp](https://neal.fun/cursor-camp/)

## Created by [Neal Agarwal](https://neal.fun/) (with contributions from [Stefan Cohen](https://stefff.com/), [Evgeniia Shmyreva](https://www.behance.net/elfinshrimp) and [Ashley Best](https://ashleybest.co.uk/))

Cursor Camp is a hand-drawn, lovingly designed interactive playground for cursors and the people who manipulate them, recreating the experience of Summer Camp in miniature, in-browser fashion. Beautifully-designed and packed with games, toys and easter eggs, part of its magic comes from the fact it is truly multiplayer; everyone visiting the camp can see everyone else who's visiting, making it a truly collaborative experience which is both fun and a reminder that the web is a collective experience which we create together.

> So honored to receive this award and glad people are enjoying the site! I grew up playing Club Penguin, and I'm happy people still yearn for silly digital spaces. Make sure to check out the other Tiny Awards nominees and thanks again to Kris, Matt and Matt for running this awesome contest!
> <span style="display:block;text-align:right">&ndash;&nbsp;Neal Agarwal</span>
